SMART UNIT SYSTEM
- Stock is maintained in one Stock Unit per product.
- Sugar recommended Stock Unit: KG.
- Milk/Oil recommended Stock Unit: Liter.
- Biscuits recommended Stock Unit: Packet.
- Count items recommended Stock Unit: Piece.

SALE EXAMPLES
- Sugar Stock Unit KG:
  Enter Quantity 250 + Unit Gram.
  App automatically deducts 0.25 KG.
  Sale rate remains rate per KG and total is calculated automatically.

PURCHASE EXAMPLE
- Sugar bag:
  Product Stock Unit = KG
  Purchase Quantity = 1
  Purchase Unit = Bag
  Qty per Pack = 50
  Total Purchase Amount = Rs 7,500
  App adds 50 KG and automatically calculates Rs 150/KG cost.
  NO manual division is required.

OTHER AUTOMATIC CONVERSIONS
- KG <-> Gram
- Liter <-> ML
- Piece <-> Dozen

CUSTOM PURCHASE PACKS
Bag / Carton / Box / Packet / Bottle / Tin / Pouch can be purchased by entering
how many Stock Units are inside one pack.

Home Use and Loss also support Gram/ML/Dozen where compatible.

Existing old transactions remain compatible because old Quantity is treated as Stock Unit quantity.
